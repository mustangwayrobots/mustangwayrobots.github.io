Get started

1

Read the club rules

Open Club Info & Guidelines and read it all before your first build.

2

Use a laptop with Chrome or Edge

Flashing works in Chrome or Edge on a Windows laptop, Mac or Chromebook. Plug the board in with the club USB-C cable.

3

Flash the firmware

Click ⚡ Flash on the project and pick your board version. When asked Erase device?, leave it unchecked to keep your saved settings.
